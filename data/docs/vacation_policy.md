# Vacation Policy

All full-time employees accrue paid vacation (PTO) every month they are employed.

- New employees (0-2 years of service) accrue 1.25 vacation days per month, which is 15 days per year.
- Employees with 3-5 years of service accrue 1.67 days per month (20 days per year).
- Employees with more than 5 years of service accrue 2.08 days per month (25 days per year).

Unused vacation days roll over to the next year, up to a maximum of 10 days.
Vacation requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance through the HR portal.
Public holidays do not count against vacation days.
