# Product FAQ

**What does the company sell?** Three product lines: Atlas (project management software),
Beacon (analytics dashboards) and Compass (customer support ticketing).

**How is pricing structured?** Each product is sold per seat per month. Atlas costs $12 per seat,
Beacon costs $25 per seat and Compass costs $18 per seat. Annual plans get a 15% discount.

**Is there a free trial?** Yes, every product has a 14-day free trial.
