# Expense Policy

Employees may be reimbursed for reasonable business expenses.

- Meals while traveling: up to $60 per day.
- Hotels: up to $200 per night in most cities, $300 per night in high-cost cities.
- Flights: economy class for trips under 6 hours; premium economy allowed for longer flights.
- Home office equipment: a one-time allowance of $500 for new employees.

Submit receipts within 30 days through the expense tool. Expenses over $1,000 need manager approval.
