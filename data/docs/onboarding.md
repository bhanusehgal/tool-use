# Onboarding Guide

Welcome! Your first week looks like this:

1. Day 1: laptop setup, accounts, and meeting your onboarding buddy.
2. Day 2: security training (mandatory) and an overview of the product.
3. Days 3-5: shadowing your team and picking up a first starter task.

New employees have a 90-day probation period with check-ins at 30, 60 and 90 days.
Order your home office equipment in your first month using the $500 allowance (see the expense policy).
