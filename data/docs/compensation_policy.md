# Compensation Policy

Salaries are reviewed once a year in March.

For the current review cycle the company approved a standard raise of 4% for all employees
whose performance rating is "meets expectations" or higher. Employees rated "exceeds expectations"
receive an additional 2% on top of the standard raise (6% total).

Raises take effect on April 1st. Bonuses are paid separately and are not part of base salary.
Salary bands for each level are published on the HR portal.
